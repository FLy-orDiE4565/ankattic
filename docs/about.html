What is Ankattic?

Created in 2026 in a bedroom somewhere, Ankattic aims to serve as an open, accessible database of math problems (with solutions, of course!)

Similar to websites like chess.com and codeforces, Ankattic intends to provide free access to math problems of all sorts, ranging from elementary times tables to PhD-level proofs. In doing so, we hope to provide equal opportunity to people across to world to not only help students of all ages practice towards improvement in math class, but also to dive into what makes mathematics truly beautiful and fun.

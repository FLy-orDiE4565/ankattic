# ankattic
<p>Online, free, and accessible database for all kinds of math and logic puzzles, questions, and problems. Currently in alpha stages.</p>
**#Horizons**
<h2>What exactly does that mean?</h2>
<p>Similar to websites like chess.com and codeforces, Ankattic intends to provide free access to math problems of all sorts, ranging from elementary times tables to PhD-level proofs. In doing so, we hope to provide equal opportunity to people across to world to not only help students of all ages practice towards improvement in math class, but also to dive into what makes mathematics truly beautiful and fun.<p>
<br>
**By math lovers, for math lovers.**
<br>
<h2>How will this work?</h2>
<p>On the problems page, problemsets from various sources will be compiled onto the website's library, and be available for users to practice (in a similar way to a Khan Academy lesson). For many questions with a singular answer, the answer will either be selectable from a range of options or submittable into a text-box. For higher level questions, such as proofs, a bigger text box will appear for the user to submit their answer. Alternatively, users will be able to upload pictures of their work, and anything submitted will go to a future staff for grading. In the future, we hope to train a machine learning model only on mathematics content to be able to grade questions, but that is simply a prospect, as of now.
However, in the near future, a log-in and profile system will be implemented for users to track their progress on Ankattic.
</p>
